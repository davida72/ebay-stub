# Iconic The Smiths Aragon Chicago Friday Aug 15 1986 Ticket Stub Queen Is Dead

The Smiths
at the Aragon Ballroom
RARE TICKET STUB
Budweiser/Jam Present
Laurence and Broadway, Chicago
Friday August 15 1986 8:00pm
General Admission
What a night!!! And yes, he did dance around with flowers!
Please note: there is a small piece of cello tape at the top of the ticket, where it was affixed into a scrap book. Please see my other listings for other concerts I also personally attended in the 1980s!
The live recording of "There Is a Light That Never Goes Out" from this exact show was released as part of The Queen Is Dead reissue bonus material.
This was mid-way through their Queen Is Dead US tour, which ran through Canada and the US that summer (Toronto area July 30–31, Ottawa/Montreal early August, then on through the Midwest and eventually to LA's Universal Amphitheatre on August 26).
The Queen Is Dead is widely regarded as one of the greatest albums ever made (regularly ranked by NME, Rolling Stone, etc.), and this tour is the one most associated with that album.
