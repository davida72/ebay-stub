# The Smiths Concert Ticket Stub Fine Art Print A4

The Smiths Concert Ticket Stub Fine Art Print A4. Condition is New. Dispatched with Royal Mail 2nd Class.
The Smiths, Free Trade Hall, Manchester, Thursday 30th October 1986 fine art ticket stub, printed on 260gsm satin paper using lifelong UV stable inks.
The Smiths last night ticket for their Queen is Dead Tour from the Free Trade Hall Manchester on 30th October 1986 and There 2nd Last Gig Ever and their last in Manchester.
The paper size is A4 with a 15mm border and a 3mm bleed, ready to go into frame (the frame is not included).
These artworks are originally hand painted with the use of acrylic paints and stencil for parts. Tickets have been painted using the original ticket stubs from back in the day.
Once finished, the painted ticket was professionally scanned and the prints are reproduced from the original.  A drop shadow is placed post scanning to make the ticket stub stand out from the paper ensuring the highest quality and finish.
