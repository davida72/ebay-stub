# Smiths New Order Festival Of The Tenth Manchester 1986 Concert Ticket Stub (UK)

Item Description
An original concert ticket stub for the all-day finale of the Festival Of The Tenth held at the Greater Manchester Exhibition Centre on the 19
th
July 1986. It is numbered Block J, Row B, Seat 15.  The festival was organized by Factory Records to celebrate the 10th anniversary of the Sex Pistols' legendary 1976 gig at the Lesser Free Trade Hall. The headliners included The Smiths, New Order, The Fall, Cabaret Voltaire, A Certain Ratio and Orchestral Manoeuvres In The Dark (OMD). Other performers included Pete Shelley, Wayne Fontana & The Mindbenders, John Cale, John Cooper Clarke, Sandie Shaw and many others.  The ticket stub measures 7cm x 10cm (2.75 inches x 3.93 inches). It has been previously folded and has large creases on the corners and across the surface. The top corners have pin holes and the back of the ticket has some discolouration. The condition is very good minus.
