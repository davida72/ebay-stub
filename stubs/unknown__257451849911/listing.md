# Rory Gallagher Concert Ticket Stubs 1977 (Calling Card, Johnny Marr, The Smiths)

I’m selling off some of my music memorabilia collection, accumulated over a number of years.
I plan to list more items in the weeks ahead.
These concert tickets are from Manchester Free Trade Hall, the site of many groundbreaking concerts by Bob Dylan, David Bowie, the Sex Pistols, Blondie, KISS, Led Zeppelin, Pink Floyd, The Rolling Stones, Van Morrison, T. Rex, Thin Lizzy, Rory Gallagher,
The Ramones,
and many others.
Rory Gallagher
January 17, 1977 @ Manchester Free Trade Hall
This is a pair of concert ticket stubs (F21 & F23), each measuring approx. 1.75” x 3".
These stubs are green. I also have a pair of orange ticket stubs listed.
Condition is Very Good, with a round purchase date stamp and a staple hole
on each ticket (see photos).
This show was part of Rory's
Calling
Card
album tour (see last photo for promo tour ad,
not
included in this listing).
Reportedly, The Smiths guitarist Johnny Marr attended this FTH show as one of his first-ever gigs, gatecrashing the event with a friend.
Marr has described his bedroom as a shrine to Rory Gallagher when he was a teenager.
1977 was a high-energy year for Gallagher, often highlighted by fans as a peak era for his live performances. The next day (Jan 18) he put on a memorable performance captured on the LP
Live At The Hammersmith Odeon
