# The Smiths Meat Is Murder Tour 1985 Concert Programme Ticket Stubs Pin Badges UK

Item Description
A collection of items from The Smith’s Meat Is Murder tour in 1985 including a concert programme, two concert ticket stubs for their performance at the Royal Court Theatre, Liverpool on the 27
th
